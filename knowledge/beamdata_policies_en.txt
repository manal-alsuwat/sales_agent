# beamdata — Customer Service Policies

---

## 1. Technical Support

- Technical support is available Sunday to Thursday, 9AM–6PM (Riyadh Time).
- For urgent issues outside working hours, submit a ticket at: support@beamdata.ai
- Standard ticket response time: 24 business hours.
- Critical issues (Priority 1) are resolved within 4 hours.

---

## 2. Subscriptions & Billing

- Monthly and annual subscription plans are available.
- Annual subscription saves 20% compared to monthly billing.
- Plan upgrades can be done anytime; the difference is calculated automatically.
- Cancellation requires 7 days notice before the renewal date.
- Invoices are automatically sent to the registered email at the start of each billing cycle.
- For detailed invoices or payment updates, contact: billing@beamdata.ai

---

## 3. Data Analytics Services

- beamdata provides interactive dashboards for real-time data analysis.
- The platform supports data import from: Excel, CSV, Google Sheets, SQL, APIs.
- Reports can be exported in: PDF, Excel, PNG formats.
- Data refreshes automatically every 15 minutes on paid plans.

---

## 4. AI Solutions

- beamdata offers custom AI models trained on client data.
- Custom model development takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on data size.
- The solution includes: data cleaning, model building, testing, and deployment.
- Post-deployment support is included for 3 months within the package.

---

## 5. Cloud Data Storage

- beamdata provides secure cloud storage encrypted with AES-256.
- Automatic daily backups are available.
- Deleted data can be recovered within 30 days of deletion.
- Storage limits by plan: Starter 50GB, Pro 500GB, Enterprise unlimited.

---

## 6. Privacy & Data Protection

- beamdata complies with Saudi PDPL and international GDPR standards.
- Client data is never shared with third parties without explicit consent.
- Clients have the right to export or delete their data at any time.

---
